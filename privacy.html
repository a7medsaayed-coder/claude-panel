Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

What we collect. At sign-up: your name and email, and your phone number and company or role if you enter them. With Google sign-in we receive only your name and email. About usage: the panel, Premiere Pro and Claude Code versions, operating system and computer name, first and last time the panel was opened, and how many times it was opened, messages sent and tools run.
